# BOMBANANA IRL — Game Guide / دليل اللعبة

*A printable, player-facing manual. Keep it on the table next to the console.*

**Credits:** BOMBANANA! — 3-player party game by **Lefto Studio**.
This physical adaptation for the IRL build is by **Monzer Taher**.

---

## Objective / الهدف

Defuse the console before the timer runs out. **Survive all 5 rounds with fewer than 3 strikes.** That's it. The console is the one place everyone can see what's happening — but each player can only offer one sense to the team.

انقد وحدة التحكم قبل انتهاء الوقت. **اعبر الـ 5 جولات بأقل من 3 ضربات.** لا تشترط خاطئة من أكثر من لاعب — المهم الانضباط.

**Timer:** 2:30 minutes. **Lives:** 3 strikes — on the 3rd, it goes **BOOM!**.

---

## The Three Players / اللاعبون الثلاثة

| Role / الدور | Sense / الحاسة | Job / المهمة |
|---|---|---|
| **The Defuser** (blind) — النازع | Touch فقط | Pulls the wires, flips the switches — touch, nothing else |
| **The Lookout** (deaf) — الشاهد | Sight فقط | Watches the screen + lamp + LEDs, talks? NO — they can't hear, so they call out with hands/notes |
| **The Analyst** (mute) — المحلل | Reads فقط | Holds this guide, knows exactly which wire to pull from the tables below, tells the team — without speaking |

Everyone talks at once = chaos. The console only counts when the rules are followed.

---

## Setup / البداية

1. Place the console in the middle of the table, screen facing up.
2. Sit down in your roles. Blindfolded? Not required — just keep your eyes on your job (Rule #1: **no one watches the screen except the Lookout**).
3. Press the **START** button on the front. The timer starts from 2:30 immediately.

---

## Cable Rounds / جولات الأسلاك

The console shows:

- **Round number:** `CABLE n/5`
- **Wires:** 3 or 4
- **Lamp colour:** RED / GREEN / BLUE
- **Screen footer:** `PLUG ALL WIRES` (until every wire is in) then `PULL ONE WIRE`

### Procedure / الخطوات

1. **Every wire starts connected (plugged in).** The Lookout checks `PLUG ALL WIRES` — that means each wire is in its socket. If something is out, someone must re-plug it before anything works.
2. The Analyst **reads this guide** and figures out which wire to cut from the lamp colour + wire count:

| Wires | RED | GREEN | BLUE |
|---|---|---|---|
| **3 wires** (من واحد إلى 3) | cut the **3rd** wire | cut the **1st** wire | cut the **2nd** wire |
| **4 wires** (من واحد إلى 4) | cut the **4th** | cut the **3rd** | cut the **2nd** |

3. The Analyst communicates the answer — **without speaking**: finger-count the positions (shows 3 fingers for the 3rd wire), point at the printed number below each socket, write it on paper.
4. The Defuser, **by touch alone**, pulls out the right wire.
5. Correct wire → round passes, next round starts.
6. Wrong wire → **strike** (+1 X). Three strikes → **BOOM!** The round is over either way — the next round starts fresh (with new wires, new lamp colour).

### Re-plug rule / قاعدة إعادة الوصل

After every cable round — win or strike — **every wire must be re-plugged** before the next round arms. The screen will show `PLUG ALL WIRES` until all of them are in, then switch to `PULL ONE WIRE`.

---

## Switch Rounds / جولات السويتشات

The console shows:

- **Round number:** `SWITCH n/5`
- **Four switches:** each displays **UP** or **DN**
- **MATCH** counter: `MATCH n/4`
- The four green LEDs = one LED per switch, lit when that switch is on its target position.
- **Time is the pressure — there are no strikes for switch rounds.**

### Procedure / الخطوات

1. Nobody at the table knows the pattern by heart — that's the point of the puzzle.
2. The Lookout watches the screen; for each switch they can see whether it's green (correct) or orange (wrong), and the `MATCH n/4` counter.
3. The Lookout tells the Defuser (through hand gestures, notes, or taps on the table): "all four match", or which switches are still orange.
4. The Defuser flips one switch at a time until `MATCH 4/4` lights up green.
5. **The pattern must stay matched for 500 ms** — against which bouncing contacts don't count — then the round passes.
6. The Analyst uses the time to catch up on the coffee. They have no job this round.

> **If the blind player flips a switch too quickly mid-bounce, the 500 ms hold absorbs it.** No emergency, no strike — just keep the switches still and wait.

## Summary Table / ملخص الجولات

| # | Round type | Strikes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cable *(always one of the two types)* | possible |
| 2 | Cable or switch *(random order each game)* | possible for cable |
| 3 | Cable or switch | possible for cable |
| 4 | Cable or switch | possible for cable |
| 5 | Cable or switch | possible for cable |

- **5 rounds total, 2 of which are always switch rounds** — the order is shuffled every game, so you can't memorise it.
- 3 wire rounds + 2 switch rounds (in some order).
- No strikes apply to switch rounds.

---

## Winning & Losing / الفوز والخسارة

- **DEFUSED** → all 5 rounds cleared in under 2:30 with fewer than 3 strikes. The console flashes green.
- **BOOM!** → timer hit 0, or third strike arrived first. The console flashes red.
- **Rematch:** press **START** — everything resets.
